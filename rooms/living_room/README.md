# Living room — current package v4

Room 003 retains its furnished interior and original EGA details. The radio artwork follows the arched casing silhouette. The left doorway connects to the hall, which owns its moving leaf. Room depth and unseen surfaces are inferred from the source artwork.

## Files and rebuild

- `build.py` and `room.json`: current geometry recipe, ports, navigation dimensions and bake settings.
- `generated/blender/living_room/living_room_source_v4.blend`: editable source scene.
- `generated/blender/living_room/living_room_v4.blend`: baked Blender scene, including shared doors for previews.
- `generated/models/rooms/living_room_v4.glb`: room export; its compact GLB and gzip sidecar are generated by sync.
- `generated/previews/rooms/living_room/`: current room views; `generated/reports/rooms/living_room/shading_report_v4.json` records the bake.

```bash
python3 scripts/build.py room living_room
python3 scripts/build.py check
./walkthrough.sh build
```

The original EGA colors and soft baked vertex lighting work without browser GI. Standard doors use the [shared kit](../../shared/doors/README.md). Interactive doors start closed; E, click or touch opens them. Room placement and connections live in `house/layout.json`.

Superseded design iterations were removed. Keep the active files named in `room.json`; see [architecture](../../docs/architecture.md) for the workflow.

The right double doors now open into the library shell. The living room owns both mirrored shared leaves; the right wall and skirting leave the full doorway clear.

## Source and generated files

This folder contains only the builder, authored `room.json` and documentation. Original artwork is in `source/`. Generated scenes live in `generated/blender/living_room/`; GLBs in `generated/models/rooms/`; previews in `generated/previews/rooms/living_room/`; derived door metadata and shading reports in `generated/reports/rooms/living_room/`. Builders never write derived data back into `room.json`.

## Artwork detail pass

Both windows have real wall apertures and recessed wooden reveals. The original
curtain contours, ties, sash and blue broken-pane linework remain source-pixel
geometry; the black pane interiors open onto enclosed black night spaces beyond
the wall. No stars or additional exterior scenery are invented. Wallpaper stops
at the openings, and the windows remain non-traversable.

The sofa has three columns of tufting and a brown/gold front rail. The radio's
arched source face is retained; its cabinet top is brown. The landscape frame
sits against the wallpaper. Candle flames stay bright and a soft local bake at
the chandelier adds light to the room, with reduced general fill. All lighting
remains static vertex color; no browser lights are required.

The optimized room is about 2.01 MB, or 632 KB with gzip. The download check keeps
a 700 KB compressed budget for this furnished room.

The inferred opposite wall continues this room’s architectural finish. See the
[complete wall-finish audit](../../docs/opposite_walls.md) for the specific treatment
and the distinction between repeating decoration and unique source details.
